Behind the story

A luxury apartment beside Gulshan Lake

Credence Arkology by Credence Housing Ltd. is an exclusive residential project beside Gulshan Lake in Gulshan‑1, Dhaka. Designed for those who value luxury, privacy, spaciousness and a peaceful lifestyle, it combines the beauty of nature with the convenience of modern city living.

Built on 10.53 kathas of land, this 15‑storey building features only one apartment per floor. Each apartment offers approximately 3,366 sq. ft. of living space plus an approximately 27 sq. ft. planter area. With 14 apartments and 24 car parking spaces, the project is planned for comfortable, spacious family living.

Surrounded by lush greenery and refreshing lake views, Arkology offers a serene environment away from the city’s bustle, while shopping malls, fine dining and business districts stay within easy reach.
